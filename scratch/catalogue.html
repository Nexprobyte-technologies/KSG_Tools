Product Categories & Overview

K.S.G. Tools (Estd. 2009) is a leading industrial tool manufacturer based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We specialize in precision-engineered industrial cutting knives, replacement shredder blades, granulator blades, sheet metal press tool dies, and custom-designed shredding machines.

All blades are manufactured from premier alloy steels including AISI D2, HCHCr (D3), High Speed Steel (HSS), and EN-31, vacuum hardened and precision ground to 56–62 HRC for maximum wear resistance, edge retention, and impact toughness.

Engineering & Manufacturing Capabilities

High-Grade Tool Steels

AISI D2, HCHCr D3, EN-31, and carbide-tipped alloys selected for extreme impact resistance and extended cutting lifespan.

Controlled Heat Treatment

Precision vacuum furnace hardening and multi-stage tempering achieving uniform 58–62 HRC hardness without surface brittleness.

VMC & CNC Machining

4-Axis VMC milling and precision CNC surface grinding ensuring ±0.01mm tolerance on mounting holes and cutting edges.

Dynamic Balancing

Individual and gang-balancing of multi-blade shredder assemblies to eliminate machine vibration and premature shaft bearing wear.

Custom Tool Sampling

Rapid reverse engineering, sample development, and custom profiling based on physical worn samples or CAD drawings.

All-India Road Dispatch

Carefully oiled, rust-proof wrapped, and wooden-pallet packed for safe transit via road transport from Coimbatore across India.
